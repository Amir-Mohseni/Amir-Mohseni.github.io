  • Uncertainty-Aware Legal Query Classification with Small Language Models

    Bachelor thesis, Maastricht University · MARBLE Research Program · Supervisor: Gerasimos Spanakis

    Investigated overconfidence in RLVR-trained LLMs for legal-query routing, and how abstention can restore a model's ability to express uncertainty. Compared encoder classifiers and small LLMs trained with supervised fine-tuning and RLVR; a 4B-parameter model improved legal-guidance agreement with frontier-model labels from 75.7% to 91.2%.

  • Reasoning Router

    Technical blog · Models

    Router that selects thinking or non-thinking inference per query. Reduces token usage by nearly 50% while staying within 5% of the best model's accuracy.
